## Sustainability rationale (as a thesis, not a result)

The project's stated path to sustainability combines: continued grant and
donation support during early stages, an agent-network fee model as
adoption grows, and education-related partnerships. **Status: thesis,
`PLANNED` — no revenue figures are attached to this thesis in this
edition.**
