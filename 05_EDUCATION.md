# 05 — Education

Education is one of CeloHT's three public pillars: Web3, financial
literacy, and digital security education, delivered primarily in Haitian
Creole. **Status: `PROJECT-REPORTED`.**

## What this book asserts

- The Education pillar exists as a documented program area across multiple
  public repositories (`CeloHT`, `celoht-docs`, `celoht-research`).

## What this book does not assert

- Specific enrollment, completion, or certification numbers are not stated
  here unless a dated, sourced figure is available (see
  `docs/22_METRICS.md`). A number such as "N people educated" should never
  be published without a status label (`verified` / `reported` /
  `estimated` / `planned`).
- Whether courses are currently published and live, vs. planned, is
  `EXTERNAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED` for this edition — confirm against the
  live product or `celoht-docs` before citing.

## Structure (as described)

- Web3 fundamentals
- Financial literacy
- Digital payments literacy
- Digital security practices
- Entrepreneurship education

Course-by-course detail should live in `celoht-docs` and be linked from
here once independently confirmed, rather than duplicated.
