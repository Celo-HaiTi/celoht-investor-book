# 01 — Identity & Mission

## Canonical identity

- **Project name:** CeloHT (never "Celo-HT")
- **GitHub organization:** `Celo-HaiTi` (never `Celo-HT`)
- **Currency terminology:** USDm (never cUSD, in current materials)
- **Gas/governance asset:** CELO
- **Founder:** Johnny Dubic, role: Founder

## Mission

CeloHT is an open-source Haitian Web3 initiative focused on financial
inclusion, blockchain education, digital payments, and entrepreneurship
within the Celo ecosystem. **Status: `PROJECT-REPORTED`.**

## Public pillars

1. **Education** — Web3, financial literacy, and digital security education
2. **Agent Network** — community-rooted agents providing cash-to-USDm
   conversion and digital payment support
3. **Reforestation** — measurable, transparently reported environmental
   restoration linked to community development

No additional pillar is recognized in this edition. Any material referring
to a fourth pillar should be treated as non-canonical until verified.

## What CeloHT is not

- Not a blockchain
- Not a cryptocurrency
- Not a native token
- Not an investment product
- Not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Celo
  Foundation or Valora, unless independently verified

## Terminology maintenance

Repository visibility and wiki content can change over time. Maintainers
should periodically re-confirm that the flagship repository and its wiki
consistently use "CeloHT" / "Celo-HaiTi" / "USDm" as the current
terminology, since older material can persist in less-visited pages (e.g.
wiki history) even after the root documentation has been updated.
