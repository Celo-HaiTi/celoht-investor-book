organized by verification category rather than by date, since specific
target dates were not independently confirmed during this rebuild.

- Wallet ecosystem support (MiniPay, Valora, WalletConnect-compatible
  wallets) remaining stable and compatible
- Independent audit firm engagement and scheduling

No item in this roadmap moves categories without a dated, sourced update
recorded in [evidence/VERIFICATION_STATUS.md](../evidence/VERIFICATION_STATUS.md).
