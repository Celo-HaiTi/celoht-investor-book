# 02 — The Problem

CeloHT's stated rationale addresses three linked gaps:

1. **Financial access** — limited traditional banking infrastructure and
   high-cost remittance/payment rails in Haiti.
2. **Digital and financial literacy** — low exposure to safe practices for
   digital payments, wallets, and blockchain-based tools.
3. **Environmental degradation** — deforestation pressure linked to
   economic hardship, which the project links to its reforestation pillar
   as a community-development intervention rather than a standalone
   environmental program.

**Status: this is a stated project thesis (`PROJECT-REPORTED`).** This book
does not cite independent macroeconomic or environmental research to
validate the scale of these problems; a due-diligence reviewer should treat
quantitative claims about Haiti's financial-inclusion gap or deforestation
rate as requiring independent, dated sourcing before use in any external
communication.
