# 07 — Reforestation

Reforestation is CeloHT's third public pillar: measurable, transparently
reported environmental restoration linked to community development.
**Status: mechanism `PROJECT-REPORTED`; impact figures `EXTERNAL
VERIFICATION REQUIRED` unless individually sourced.**

## Required separation of terms

This book distinguishes three distinct states, and no reforestation claim
in this edition collapses them:

- **Financed** — funds have been allocated toward reforestation activity
- **Planted** — trees have physically been planted
- **Verified** — an independent or documented verification process has
  confirmed planting and survival

## What this edition does not include

- No fabricated tree counts
- No "impact dashboard" figures, since none were independently confirmed
  as part of a live product surface during this rebuild

## Smart contract support

`celoht-smart-contracts` documents a Reforestation-related contract module
as part of its testnet stack, which supports the mechanism-level design
claim above. It does not by itself confirm a specific quantity of trees
financed, planted, or verified.

## Recommendation for future editions

If CeloHT publishes a reforestation report with dated, sourced figures
(financed vs. planted vs. verified, with geography), it should be linked
here and mirrored into [docs/22_METRICS.md](./22_METRICS.md) with a verification status.
